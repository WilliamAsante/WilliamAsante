A word from the Minister

Wherever you are coming from, there is a seat for you here.

Mpraeso sits high on the Kwahu ridge, where the morning mist rolls over the hills. Our congregation has gathered here for generations to sing, to hear the Word and to carry one another in prayer.

Whether you grew up Presby, are home for Easter, or are visiting a church for the first time, we would love to meet you. Stay after service, share a handshake, and let us know your name.

Nec tamen consumebatur

“The bush was burning, yet it was not consumed.”Exodus 3:2

The burning bush is the emblem of Presbyterian churches around the world. Our own story in Ghana grew from the Basel Mission, whose missionaries reached the Gold Coast in 1828 and later carried the gospel up into Kwahu.

A ministry for every generation

Every member belongs to a generational group, so no one walks alone. Find yours below and come to the next meeting.

Children's Service

Ages 3–11 · Sundays 9:30 AM

Bible stories, songs and memory verses in the children's hall, led by trained teachers.

Junior Youth

Ages 12–17 · Sundays after service

Growing faith, character and friendship through the teenage years, with camps and quiz nights.

Young People's Guild

Ages 18–30 · Sundays 4:00 PM

The YPG: worship, Bible study, outreach and leadership for students and young workers.

Young Adults' Fellowship

Ages 30–40 · Fortnightly

The YAF: faith for careers, marriage and raising a family, with friends at the same stage.

Women's Fellowship

Tuesdays 4:00 PM

Prayer, welfare and service. The women of the church who hold this congregation together.

Men's Fellowship

Second Saturday · 7:00 AM

Breakfast, the Word and brotherhood. Men encouraging men to lead at home and in the town.

Church Choir

Rehearsals Thursdays 6:30 PM

Hymns and anthems in four parts. New voices are always welcome; no audition for the first month.

Singing Band

Rehearsals Saturdays 4:00 PM

Joyful Twi and English choruses with drums and clapping, the heartbeat of the second service.

Bible Study & Prayer Group

Wednesdays & Fridays 6:30 PM

The BSPG keeps the church on its knees, leading Bible study, revival nights and prayer chains.

    What should I wear?

    Whatever you'd wear to visit family. Many members dress in their Sunday best or kente, but come as you are.

    Which service is in English?

    The 7:00 AM First Service is in English. The 9:30 AM Second Service is in Twi, with Bible readings in both.

    What about my children?

    Children aged 3–11 join Children's Service during the 9:30 AM service. Teenagers sit with Junior Youth.

    Is there parking?

    Yes, there is parking in the church compound. Ushers will help you find a space and a seat.

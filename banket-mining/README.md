# Banket Reef Mining: landing page (concept)

A landing page for a fictional open-pit gold miner in Tarkwa, Western Region, Ghana.
Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Serve from the repo root (`python3 -m http.server`) and open `/banket-mining/`.

## Brief (written by us)

- **Client:** Banket Reef Mining Ltd, a Ghanaian-led, mid-tier open-pit gold producer working the Tarkwaian banket reefs (fictional company).
- **Audiences:** investors and partners, job seekers from host communities, community members and regulators.
- **Goals:** look credible and technical, prove responsible mining (rehabilitation, water, community), recruit locally, collect investor and community enquiries.
- **Tone:** confident and plain-spoken. Geology as the design system.

## The layout idea

The page is a drill hole. Each section is a rock layer from the real Tarkwa sequence: laterite, Huni sandstone, Tarkwa phyllite and the banket reef. A depth gauge on the left counts metres as you scroll down to the reef, then back up to the surface for rehabilitation and careers.

- Hero: illustrated cross-section with an animated haul truck climbing the pit benches and a working drill rig.
- Each layer has a sticky rock-core sample and log label beside its content.
- Operations: a pinned, sideways "rock to bar" track driven by vertical scroll.
- People: figures presented as a drill log, with assay-style bars.
- The reef: dark rock full of pebbles and gold specks, lit by a headlamp that follows the cursor (it sweeps on its own on phones).
- Rehabilitation: the gauge climbs back to 0 m while seedlings grow.
- Phones: the gauge becomes a pill that opens a list of layers to jump to.
- Reduced motion: no pinning or sweeping, just fades.

All figures, names and contacts are placeholders for a fictional company.

Country & Community · 610 m

We work on Country, and we answer to it.

01 · Partnership

Traditional Owners at the table

Seven land-use agreements with the Banjima, Martu and Ngadju peoples — covering heritage protection, jobs, business contracts and shared decision-making from exploration through closure.

02 · Rehabilitation

Closure designed on day one

6,100 hectares progressively rehabilitated with native seed collected on-site. Every pit and waste landform has a closure plan, independently reviewed every three years.

03 · Safety

Everyone home, every shift

Our critical-control programme, fatigue monitoring and 24/7 remote operations centre have cut recordable injuries by 62% in five years. The target remains zero.

# Banket Reef Mining v2: "The Pit"

A redesign of `banket-mining/` with a new layout and an Envato Elements media plan. The company is fictional and every figure is illustrative.
Serve from the repo root (`python3 -m http.server`) and open `/banket-pit/`.

## The layout idea

The page is an open pit, seen in section.

- Each section is a **bench** that steps inward, so the content column narrows as you scroll down. Sloped bench faces and dashed hi-vis safety berms sit between the benches.
- The **pit floor** is the narrowest point and holds the gold: production figures.
- After the floor the benches **step back out** while you read about rehabilitation and community. The page ends wide again at the surface with careers and contact.
- A **haul truck** drives a ramp down the left pit wall as you scroll, crosses the floor, and climbs out up the right wall (desktop).
- The header shows a live **RL (reduced level)**, the elevation in metres, which falls as you descend and rises as you climb out.
- Operations has a **split-flap shift board** that flips into place: rock to doré in 36 hours.
- Until real media is added, the hero and every photo slot show **generated contour maps**. The hero's contours drift slowly.

### Free fallback photos (live now)

Until the Envato files are added, these spots use free Unsplash photos (free for commercial use): hero aerial of a mining area (Curioso Photography), bare rocky ground from the air (Patrick Hendry), front loader (Marek Novotný), worker in a hard hat (unknown, Unsplash ID eCfCIwtKdsg), furnace plant pipework (Aranka Sinnema) and a seedling in soil (Markus Spiske). The *Mine planning*, gold bars and borehole spots stay as contour maps until real photos are added; the free library had nothing suitable.

The Envato items above were chosen from search results by their titles; the previews couldn't be viewed from the build environment. Check each one before downloading. Real photos from a client's own site would beat stock every time.

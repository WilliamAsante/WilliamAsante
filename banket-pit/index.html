Bench 01RL 290Who we are

We started with one pit and a borrowed drill rig.

That was 2011. Today Banket Reef runs two open pits and a 4.8 million-tonne-a-year mill west of Tarkwa town, with a team of more than 1,800 people. Most of them grew up within an hour of the site.

We are a mid-tier producer, and we like it that way: big enough to invest in safety, training and closure; small enough that the general manager knows the drill crews by name.

  • Founded 2011
  • Two open pits
  • 4.8 Mt/yr mill
  • 11-year reserve life

Why it tastes different

  • Flour from a local mill

    Stone-ground wheat and rye, delivered fresh every week.

  • 48-hour sourdough

    A slow ferment for flavor, a crackly crust and an open crumb.

  • Nothing from a packet

    Doughs, fillings, jams and frostings are all made here.

  • Vegan options every day

    Our breads are vegan, plus at least one pastry and one cookie.

  • Baked all morning

    Batches come out from 6:30 am, so there's always something warm.

  • No bread goes to waste

    What's left at closing goes to the community fridge down the road.

About us

Sunday Oven started with one deck oven, a borrowed kitchen and a sourdough starter we've kept alive ever since.

Today we bake for the neighborhood six days a week, using the same slow methods. Come in for a loaf, stay for a coffee, and say hello to whoever's on the counter.

How ordering works

  1. Send your request with the form, at least two days ahead.

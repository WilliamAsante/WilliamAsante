About us

Sunday Oven started with one deck oven, a borrowed kitchen and a sourdough starter we've kept alive ever since. Today we bake for the neighborhood six days a week, with the same slow methods.

  • Flour from a local mill. Stone-ground wheat and rye, delivered every week.
  • Nothing from a packet. Doughs, fillings, jams and frostings are all made here.
  • No bread goes to waste. What's left at closing goes to the community fridge down the road.

Cakes and big orders

Birthday cakes, pastry boxes for the office, bread for a wedding. Tell us what you need and we'll make it.

  1. Send your request with the form. Give us at least two days' notice.
  2. We confirm by email within one working day, with the price and any questions.
  3. Pick it up at the counter on the day. Payment is taken at pickup.

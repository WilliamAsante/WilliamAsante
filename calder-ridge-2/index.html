02Site life

Twenty-four hours in the Ridgeback pit

  1. 05:00

    Handover under lights

    Day crews walk the plant with night shift and sign the safety brief.

  2. 08:30

    Drill and blast

    168 holes on the second bench. Nobody fires until everyone is accounted for.

  3. 11:00

    Water carts and dust

    Haul roads are wetted on a loop. Monitors report dust every ten minutes.

  4. 13:00

    Haul and crush

    Eight trucks carry ore up the ramp to the crusher. Every load is weighed.

  5. 18:15

    Wall inspection

    Survey prisms on the rim are read as the light drops.

  6. 22:00

    Night shift

    Lighting towers go up and Perth takes over the driverless fleet.

The bar under the hours is daylight at Ridgeback: sunrise 05:08, sunset 18:08.

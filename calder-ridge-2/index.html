Three mines. One safety standard.

Our mines sit in three very different parts of Western Australia: the iron ranges of the Pilbara, the gold belts of the Goldfields and the lithium pegmatites of the South West. We run them the same way: plan the closure first, protect heritage first, and send everyone home safe.

A day on site

05:00
5:00 am

Shift handover under lights

Crews walk the plant with the night team, review overnight faults and sign the safety brief before a single machine moves.

8:30 am

Drill and blast

Rigs mark out the next bench. Exclusion zones are set by radar and cleared by hand, and nobody fires until every person is accounted for.

1:00 pm

Haul, crush, rail

Autonomous trucks move ore to the crusher while operators in Perth watch every load. Water carts keep dust down on the road.

6:15 pm

Dusk inspection

Geotechnical teams check pit walls as the light drops. Anything that moved gets reported, flagged and fenced before the night shift arrives.

Leaving it better

Every pit has a closure plan before the first blast. We collect native seed on site, replace topsoil in the order we removed it, and monitor regrowth for ten years.

  • 6,100 hectares rehabilitated so far
  • Seven agreements with Traditional Owners
  • Closure plans reviewed independently every three years

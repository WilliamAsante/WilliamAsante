01OperationsWestern Australia20°–35° S

Three mines, three geologies

Banded iron in the Pilbara, gold-bearing greenstone in the Goldfields and lithium pegmatite in the South West, on three Countries up to 1,400 km apart. Each is run to the same three rules.

  1. Plan the closure firstEvery pit has an approved closure plan before the first blast.
  2. Protect heritage firstTraditional Owners survey and sign off ground before we disturb it.
  3. Send everyone homeNo production target outranks a stop-work call from anyone on site.

05A day on siteRidgeback pit30.92° S 121.61° E

A day at Ridgeback

Six moments from one roster day in the pit, placed at the hour they happen.

  1. 05:00

    Handover under lights

    Day crews walk the plant with night shift and sign the safety brief.

  2. 11:00

    Water carts and dust

    Haul roads are wetted on a loop. Boundary monitors report dust every ten minutes.

  3. 18:15

    Wall inspection

    Survey prisms on the rim are read as the light drops. Any movement is fenced off.

  1. 08:30

    Drill and blast

    168 holes on the second bench. Nobody fires until everyone is accounted for.

  2. 13:00

    Haul and crush

    Eight trucks carry ore up the ramp to the crusher. Every load is weighed.

  3. 22:00

    Night shift

    Lighting towers go up and Perth takes over the driverless fleet.

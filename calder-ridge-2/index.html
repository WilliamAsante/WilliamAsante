02A day on site

A day at Ridgeback

05:00

  1. Handover under lights

    Day crews walk the plant with night shift, go through overnight faults and sign the safety brief before a machine moves.

  2. Drill and blast

    168 holes on the second bench. Exclusion zones are cleared on foot, and nobody fires until every person on the lease is accounted for.

  3. Water carts and dust

    Every haul road is wetted on a loop. Dust monitors on the lease boundary report to the control room every ten minutes.

  4. Haul and crush

    Eight trucks on the ramp carry ore up to the crusher. Every load is weighed and logged.

  5. Wall inspection

    Survey prisms around the rim are read as the light drops. Any movement is flagged and fenced before night shift.

  6. Night shift

    Lighting towers go up on the active benches, and the operations centre in Perth takes over the driverless fleet.

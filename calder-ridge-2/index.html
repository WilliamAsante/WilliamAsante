A day at Ridgeback

Fourteen hours in the Ridgeback pit, from first light to last.

  1. 05:00

    Handover under lights

    Day crews walk the plant with night shift, go through overnight faults and sign the safety brief before a machine moves.

  2. 08:30

    Drill and blast

    168 holes on the second bench. Exclusion zones are cleared on foot, and nobody fires until every person on the lease is accounted for.

  3. 13:00

    Haul and crush

    Eight trucks on the ramp carry ore up to the crusher while water carts keep the dust down. Every load is weighed.

  4. 18:15

    Wall inspection

    Survey prisms around the rim are read as the light drops. Any movement is flagged and fenced before night shift.

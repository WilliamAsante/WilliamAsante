Questions

What will I get?

A different coffee most deliveries, picked by our head roaster from what's on the roasting schedule that week. If you choose a style, we'll stick to coffees that fit it.

How fresh is it?

We roast on Tuesdays and Fridays and ship the next day, so your coffee is two to four days off roast when it arrives. That's right when it starts tasting its best.

Can I pause or skip a delivery?

Yes. In your account you can skip the next delivery, pause for one to three months, change your plan or update your address. Changes made before the shipping day apply to the next box.

How do I cancel?

From your account, any time. There's no minimum term and no cancellation fee.

Can I send it as a gift?

Gift subscriptions are coming soon. For now, you can start a plan with the recipient's shipping address and cancel it whenever you like.
